Create the Outdoor Living Space You Deserve

Hand-built projects guided by onsite attention every day

Decksteriors is a locally owned outdoor living company serving San Antonio and the Texas Hill Country. We specialize in custom deck design and installation, pergola design and installation, arbor design and installation, gazebos design and construction, screened patio enclosures and installation, and patio cover installation. Since 2005, we've helped homeowners extend their living space outdoors with structures built to last.

You can expect more than just a finished project. The owner is hands-on with every build and is typically onsite daily to oversee quality and workmanship. That level of involvement means your outdoor space gets the kind of attention you'd give your own home, with details reviewed at every stage and nothing left to chance.


Our approach is rooted in more than 30 years in the outdoor construction industry, including a decade as a subcontractor before founding the company. Decksteriors builds every structure using premium materials and durable craftsmanship, the same approach the owner would apply to his own property. Your outdoor living area is designed and constructed with standards you'll appreciate for years.

What Happens During Your Project

When you work with us, you're working directly with someone who will be onsite regularly, checking progress and ensuring each phase meets the standard we've built our reputation on. From initial consultation to final walkthrough, you'll know what's happening, when it's happening, and what comes next. We document the scope, confirm material selections, and keep you informed as your outdoor living space takes shape.


Our process is designed to give you clarity at every turn. You're not waiting for callbacks or wondering where things stand. The owner's daily involvement means questions get answered quickly, adjustments are made with care, and your project stays on course from start to finish.


Here's what sets the experience apart:

Premium materials chosen for performance and longevity in the Texas climate

Onsite supervision by the owner, ensuring workmanship aligns with your expectations

Structures built to the same standards we'd use on our own homes

A reputation backed by an A+ BBB rating and decades of hands-on outdoor construction experience

Dependable Work From a Team That Stays Involved

Decksteriors was founded in 2005 after 10 years working as a subcontractor and more than 30 years in the outdoor construction industry overall. That depth of experience shows up in how we approach each build—methodically, thoughtfully, and with an eye for what holds up over time. When you hire a locally owned company with this much history in the field, you're hiring someone who knows the work inside and out.


We don't cut corners, and we don't hand off responsibility. The owner's presence onsite means accountability is built into the timeline, not added as an afterthought. You get the benefit of that consistency from the first measurement to the final inspection, with durable craftsmanship guiding every decision along the way.
